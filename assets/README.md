# Photo

Put your photo in this folder as **`photo.jpg`** (lowercase, exactly that name).

- Square works best, at least 400 × 400 px.
- Keep it under about 300 KB so the page loads fast.
- Until the file exists, the site shows your initials instead, on both the English and French pages.

To use a PNG or another name, change `assets/photo.jpg` in `index.html` and `../assets/photo.jpg` in `fr/index.html`.
